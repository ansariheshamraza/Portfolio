Project Overview

RachnaX AI is a structured thinking and content generation tool built specifically for Indian students, creators, and early-stage startup founders. The platform helps transform scattered thoughts into clear, structured content ready for various platforms.

Built on AWS infrastructure using API Gateway, Lambda functions, and Amazon Bedrock with Claude 3 Haiku, the platform focuses on maintaining the user's creative voice while providing structure and clarity to their content.

The project was submitted to the AI for Bharat Hackathon and represents my exploration into building AI-powered tools that solve real problems for Indian creators and students.

Project Details

Key Features

Structured Thinking

Transform scattered thoughts and rough ideas into clear, organized content structures that maintain your creative voice.

Multi-Platform Ready

Generate content optimized for Instagram, YouTube, blogs, and other platforms with platform-specific formatting.

Indian Context

Built specifically for Indian creators with understanding of local context, culture, and content preferences.
